<b>1. Post: Security wake-up — the OTA bug that keeps coming back</b>
Two October CVEs share one root cause: firmware updates that nobody authenticated.

One device does not verify signatures before installing an image. Another fetches updates over plain HTTP with no integrity check. Both were published within two weeks of each other.

The insight for me: OTA is the most privileged code path in a device, and it is often the least reviewed. Signature verification must happen on the device, before the swap, not at the server.

<b>2. Post: Tooling unlock — FreeRTOS gets a periodic delay API</b>
FreeRTOS V11.3.1 adds xTaskPeriodicDelay, meant to replace xTaskDelayUntil for periodic tasks.

The same release adds configIDLE_AFFINITY, which pins each idle task to its core in SMP builds, and uxTaskCallForEachTask for walking every task with a callback.

Periodic timing and per-core idle behaviour are exactly where dual-core firmware gets subtle. I will test the new delay on a real loop before trusting it.

Do you use xTaskDelayUntil today, and would you switch?

<b>3. Post: Capability unlock — SMP FreeRTOS on Armv8-M</b>
FreeRTOS V11.3.1 brings symmetric multiprocessing to the Armv8-M ports for GCC, ArmClang and IAR.

It also hardens the secure-context code: stack sizes are validated at allocation and the currently loaded secure context cannot be freed.

Multicore plus TrustZone on a mainstream kernel means the secure side is now part of scheduler correctness, not a separate world.
