<b>═ 🔥 TOP STORY ═</b>
CVE-2026-10672 is a high-severity (CVSS 8.2) out-of-bounds read in Zephyr's LwM2M firmware pull client. The Package URI is copied into a fixed 128-byte buffer with no length check, so a 128–254 character URI leaves it unterminated and later string handling reads adjacent memory. That over-read data is appended to outbound CoAP requests, leaking device memory to the server or an on-path attacker on a session without strong DTLS, and can also crash the device. It affects Zephyr 3.0.0 through 4.4.0 and the patch adds a length check plus a bounded, NUL-terminated copy. The original publication date was not verified, so treat it as current exposure rather than a fresh disclosure. Why it matters: any Zephyr device using LwM2M firmware pull needs the fix, and the lesson (bound every URI copy) applies to your own OTA code.

<b>1. Post: Security wake-up — a URI that was one byte too long</b>
A 128-byte buffer, a URI up to 254 characters, and no NUL terminator.

CVE-2026-10672 is an out-of-bounds read in Zephyr's LwM2M firmware pull client. The over-read bytes end up in outbound CoAP requests, so the bug leaks memory to whoever is on the other end.

What I take from it: the OTA path is the part of a device that talks to the network and handles attacker-influenced strings, and it often gets less review than the main application.

Which input in your update path have you never tried to make too long?

<b>2. Post: Regulatory shift — the CRA reporting clock is already running</b>
Since 11 September 2026, manufacturers have had to report actively exploited vulnerabilities under the Cyber Resilience Act.

Full application is 11 December 2027, so the reporting duty arrives before the rest. Trade-press sources also say a standard does not give presumption of conformity until it is cited in the Official Journal. I would verify dates against official texts.

For firmware teams that means a vulnerability intake and update process has to exist before the paperwork does.

Does your team know who would receive a report on your product today?
